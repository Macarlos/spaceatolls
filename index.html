Space Atolls — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Space Atolls ("the game") is made by Karol Macioszek ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play the game on Android.

In short: the game does not collect, send or share any personal information. Everything stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. The game:

  • has no accounts and no sign-in,
  • has no advertising and no in-app purchases,
  • has no chat and no way to contact other players,
  • does not use analytics, crash-reporting, tracking or advertising tools from us or from anyone else,
  • asks for no device permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files),
  • does not connect to the internet at all.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game keeps your progress on your device, in storage that only the game can read. This includes coins and materials, what you have built, names you type for planets, and settings such as music and sound volume. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else by the game.

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes this information.

Device backups

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the game's progress in your phone's backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps. That backup is made and stored by Google under Google's privacy policy. We cannot see or access it. You can turn backups on or off in your phone's settings.

Crash reports from Google Play

If you have agreed on your phone to share usage and diagnostics data, Google Play may send anonymous crash reports to developers through the Play Console. These reports are collected by Google, not by the game, and do not identify you.

Children

Space Atolls is made for children and families. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. We follow the Google Play Families Policy, the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) by not collecting personal data at all.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. As we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access or delete; your progress on the device can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the game. You may still contact us with any question, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority (in Poland: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO).

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how information is handled, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
